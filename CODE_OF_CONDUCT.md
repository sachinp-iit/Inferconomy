# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Inferconomy to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source
project for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and participants are expected to:

* Be respectful and professional.
* Welcome differing technical opinions.
* Give and receive constructive feedback.
* Focus discussions on ideas, implementation, research, and engineering rather
  than individuals.
* Respect privacy and confidential information.
* Encourage thoughtful technical discussion and collaboration.
* Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate
intimidation, personal attacks, publishing private information without
permission, and other conduct that would make participation unsafe or
unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a
constructive project environment.

Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project
participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainer privately rather than escalating the issue through a public
discussion.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where
participants are representing or contributing to Inferconomy.
